# Content drafts: why there are no new articles here

The brief asked for five new 1,000-1,500 word articles. Every one of those topics is already
published on ypnus.com, most at two to four times that length, with FAQ schema and internal links.
A second page on the same keyword would compete with the first in Google (keyword cannibalization)
and split its links, so writing them would lower rankings for the money keywords, not raise them.
Under the brief's own decision rule ("If NO to all 3, DO NOT do it"), they were not written.
