# Backlink and directory outreach drafts

**Status: drafts only.** Nothing in this file has been submitted, posted or sent. Every directory form and every email is for a person to review, personalize and send.

Why this exists: as of November 2026 ypnus.com has a thin backlink profile, and most of the links it does have are spam. A small number of relevant, earned links will help more than many low-quality ones. Quality and relevance come first; quantity does not matter.

All business facts below come from `src/lib/business-profile.ts` and `docs/gbp-ready-to-paste.md`, the same source as the site's schema and footer. If either changes, change this file too.

## Rules before you send anything

1. **Never pay for a link, trade links, or accept a listing that requires a reciprocal link.** Google treats those as link spam. Skip any directory that charges for a followed link.
2. **Use the same name, address and phone number everywhere** (the block below). Inconsistent details weaken local listings.
3. **Say only what the product does today.** Exclusivity applies within YPN USA (other lenders can still market in the same ZIP code). Cerebro answers, qualifies, emails check-ins and first replies (on its own by default, or after the officer approves them) and drafts posts; it sends no texts. No ranking promises, no customer counts, no results claims, no "best" or "#1".
4. **Disclose who you are** in every email: you are the founder and you are asking for a link to your own site.
5. **Follow CAN-SPAM for every outreach email**, even one-to-one: accurate sender and subject line, your postal address, and a working way to say "no thanks." Honor opt-outs within 10 business days.
6. **Send one email, and at most one follow-up** a week or more later. If there is no reply, stop.
7. **Do not ask a site to remove, rewrite or "update" an article just to place a link.** Offer something useful and let the editor decide.
8. **NMLS Consumer Access is the regulator's record, not a marketing directory.** Keep NMLS #787257 accurate on every listing; do not try to "submit" the company there.

I read your piece "[exact post title]" and liked [one specific thing you actually liked, such as how you handled the RESPA section].

David

## After a link goes live

1. Record the URL and the anchor text in the tracking sheet.
2. Thank the editor once, briefly.
3. Check the link in about a week with Search Console's Links report.
4. If the link goes to a page that has since moved, the site's redirects handle it; do not ask the editor to update it.
