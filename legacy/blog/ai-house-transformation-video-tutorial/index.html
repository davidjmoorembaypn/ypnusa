AI House Transformation Video Tutorial - Veo 3.1 Fast Tutorial and CapCut Workflow

By David J. Moore, MBA - April 11, 2026 - 7 min read

This is the exact workflow used to create viral AI house transformation and renovation animation videos for YouTube and TikTok using entirely free tools. By the end of this tutorial you will be able to produce a complete cinematic AI renovation video in under 90 minutes.

The Tools You Need (All Free)

Veo 3.1 by Google FREE
Google's most advanced AI video generator. Creates realistic 5 to 10 second clips from text prompts and image inputs. Best tool currently available for AI construction video and transformation sequences. Access at labs.google.com/flow.
Leonardo AI FREE
AI image generator specialized for architectural and interior design renders. Free tier includes 150 credits daily. Use for photorealistic after renovation images and AI home design visuals.
ElevenLabs FREE TIER
Best AI voiceover tool available. Free tier generates 10,000 characters of speech per month - enough for 4 to 6 renovation videos. Voices sound genuinely human.
CapCut FREE
Best free video editor for YouTube automation. Has built-in AI features including auto-captions, beat sync, background removal, and AI-generated transitions. Desktop and mobile versions available.
ChatGPT FREE
Script writing for renovation video narration. Free tier is sufficient for batch producing scripts for an entire week of content in one session.

Step 1 - Source Your Before Image

You need a compelling before image to anchor your transformation video. Three reliable sources work well for this:

  • Zillow or Redfin listings - Search for dated or distressed properties. Properties listed as fixer-upper or as-is almost always have dramatic visual transformation potential. Screenshots of listing photos are fine for content creation.
  • Google Street View - Search any address in a suburb and screenshot the exterior. Older neighborhoods from the 1960s through 1980s provide ideal before material.
  • AI generated images - Use Leonardo AI to generate a run-down house image if you want complete creative control. Prompt: photorealistic photograph of a neglected 1970s ranch style house with overgrown landscaping, peeling paint, and dated windows, golden hour lighting, wide angle lens.

Step 2 - Write Your Veo 3.1 Prompt

The quality of your Veo 3.1 output depends almost entirely on prompt quality. Here are proven prompt templates for AI house transformation videos:

Exterior Transformation Prompt
Cinematic timelapse transformation of a 1970s ranch house exterior. The house morphs smoothly from neglected with peeling paint and overgrown yard into a stunning modern farmhouse with fresh white paint, black trim, new windows, manicured landscaping, and a paved driveway. Warm afternoon sunlight. Photorealistic. 4K quality. Smooth morphing transition effect.
Kitchen Remodel Prompt
Cinematic before and after transformation of an outdated 1980s kitchen. Smooth morphing transition from dated yellow tile counters, oak cabinets, and old appliances into a modern open concept kitchen with white quartz countertops, navy shaker cabinets, stainless steel appliances, and pendant lighting. Bright natural light from large windows. Photorealistic 4K.
Full Home AI Renovation Timelapse Prompt
Aerial drone view timelapse of a complete home renovation. Old weathered house with damaged roof and overgrown lot transforms into a modern craftsman home with new roof, fresh landscaping, stone pathway, and warm evening lighting. Smooth continuous transformation animation. Cinematic color grade. Photorealistic.

Step 3 - Generate After Renders in Leonardo AI

While your Veo 3.1 video generates (takes 2 to 4 minutes), open Leonardo AI and generate your after renders. These are the static high-quality images used in your thumbnail and as cutaway shots in the video.

Use the PhotoReal model in Leonardo AI for maximum realism. Set image dimensions to 1792x1024 for YouTube thumbnail use. Generate at least 4 variations and pick the best 2. Prompt structure: photorealistic exterior of a newly renovated modern farmhouse, fresh white paint, black windows, manicured front yard, stone pathway, blue sky, warm afternoon light, real estate photography style.

Step 4 - Script and Voiceover

Open ChatGPT and use this exact prompt to generate your narration script:

ChatGPT Script Prompt
Write a 90 second YouTube narration script for a before and after AI home renovation video. The property is a 1970s ranch house in Phoenix Arizona that was transformed with new exterior paint, updated landscaping, kitchen remodel, and bathroom renovation. The total renovation budget was $42,000 and the estimated value increase is $95,000. Tone is enthusiastic, informative, and investment-focused. End with a call to action to use the free Renovation ROI Analyzer at ToInvested.com to calculate whether their own renovation investment pays off. Keep under 200 words.

Copy the script into ElevenLabs. Select the Rachel or Adam voice (both perform well for renovation content). Generate, download as MP3, and save to your project folder.

Step 5 - CapCut Editing Workflow

Open CapCut desktop. Create a new project at 1920x1080 for YouTube (or 1080x1920 for Shorts and TikTok). Import all your assets: Veo 3.1 transformation clip, Leonardo AI renders, and ElevenLabs voiceover MP3.

Timeline structure that maximizes watch time: Start with 2 seconds of the worst before image with dramatic music swell. Cut to a 1 second text card reading BEFORE. Show the Veo 3.1 transformation animation at full length. Cut to AFTER text card. Show your Leonardo AI renders one by one with slow zoom. Overlay voiceover throughout. End with your call to action screen showing the ToInvested Renovation ROI Analyzer URL.

For music, use CapCut's built-in royalty-free library. Search for dramatic transformation or epic reveal. The right music is responsible for at least 30 percent of viewer emotional response in renovation videos.

Add auto-captions using CapCut's AI caption feature. This alone increases average view duration by 12 to 18 percent according to creator data, because many viewers watch without sound especially on mobile.

Step 6 - Title, Thumbnail, and Upload Optimization

Title formula: [Transformation Type]: AI [Renovation Type] Turns [Before Description] Into [After Description]. Examples that consistently perform: AI Renovation Transforms Abandoned 1970s Ranch Into $400K Modern Farmhouse. Or: I Used AI to Design a Complete Home Makeover - The Results Are Incredible. Or: Before vs After: AI Home Renovation Adds $95,000 in Value for $42,000.

For your thumbnail use the strongest before image on the left and your best Leonardo AI render on the right. Add a bright versus arrow in the middle. Bold text overlay with the value add dollar amount performs well in this niche. Create thumbnail in Canva using a blank 1280x720 template.
