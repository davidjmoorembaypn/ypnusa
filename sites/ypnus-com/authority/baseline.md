ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Copilot and Google AI Overviews draw most of their citations from these same top-ranking pages, so a brand that is absent here is very unlikely to be cited in AI answers. It is not a direct reading of AI answers. A real AI-answer check needs one of the paid tools above (decision for David).

## What it means

- **YPN USA is invisible today**, including for its own brand name. Search engines don't yet connect "YPN USA" to ypnus.com, so AI tools have nothing to cite.
- **The competitors that win citations are content publishers**: BNTouch, Shape (setshape.com), LeadPops, Jungo, Addy, EmpowerLO. They win with long "best X for loan officers" lists, "X vs Y" comparisons and how-to guides, which is exactly the format this month's work upgrades.
- **Review directories** (G2, Capterra, GetApp, SourceForge, FitGap) own the "alternatives" and "vs" prompts. A free YPN USA listing on G2 and Capterra is a cheap way in (David's decision; needs a company account).
- Brand-name invisibility is the first fix: consistent Organization/Person schema, an About page that matches the founder page, and Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster verification at cutover.
