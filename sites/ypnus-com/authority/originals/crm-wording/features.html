Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does YPN USA do?

YPN USA is a mortgage growth platform for loan officers — it combines Cerebro AI, borrower lead capture and follow-up, local SEO, Realtor co-marketing, and exclusive ZIP territory into one connected system.

What is Cerebro?

Cerebro is YPN USA's AI growth assistant. It qualifies borrowers in conversation, captures their loan goal and timeline, and keeps follow-up moving automatically.

Is YPN USA a CRM?

No. YPN USA works alongside your existing CRM (Salesforce, Encompass, Velocify) — captured leads sync into your existing pipeline rather than living in a separate system.

How does ZIP protection work?

One licensed loan officer holds each ZIP on the platform. Your local visibility in that ZIP isn't shared with another MLO.

Does checking a ZIP reserve it?

No. Checking availability is free and instant and reserves nothing. A ZIP becomes protected only once you're on a paid plan.

Can I start free?

Yes. The Free plan requires no credit card and includes the Cerebro AI diagnostic and borrower intake assistant. It doesn't include a protected ZIP.

How does YPN USA work with Realtor relationships?

The Realtor co-marketing portal gives you co-branded landing pages, shared lead tracking, and outreach templates to build agent partnerships in your protected ZIP.

What happens to captured opportunities?

Cerebro qualifies each opportunity and routes it to you, with automated follow-up keeping it warm until you're ready to act on it.

Can I use YPN USA with my existing mortgage workflow?

Yes. It's designed as a growth layer alongside your existing LOS and CRM, not a replacement for them.
