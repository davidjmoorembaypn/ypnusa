Most CRMs were built for sales teams selling software, not for loan officers managing pre-approvals, conditions, and closing timelines. YPN USA's CRM tracks borrowers the way you actually think about them: by loan stage, by referral source, and by which ones need a nudge this week.

What the CRM does

  • Automated lead nurture - every new contact enters a stage-based sequence (new inquiry, pre-approval, in-process, closed, past client) instead of a flat contact list.
  • Automated email marketing - pre-built sequences for rate updates, pre-approval reminders, and past-client check-ins, editable under your own brand.
  • Mortgage-specific fields - loan type, target close date, referral source, and territory, not generic "deal stage" fields borrowed from B2B sales software.
  • One record per borrower across the funnel - from first AI intake conversation through funded loan and into the past-client database.

Built into the same system as your leads

Unlike a bolt-on CRM you'd have to connect to a separate lead source, this CRM is the same system your exclusive ZIP leads and AI intake already flow into. No CSV exports, no double entry.

Built for loan officers, not generic sales teams

Who this is for: loan officers building direct borrower demand in an exclusive ZIP territory. Who a generic CRM is for: broad sales teams managing generic opportunities, with no territory, loan-stage, or compliance context built in.

What YPN USA replaces: your contact spreadsheet, a bolt-on nurture tool, and manual lead-to-CRM entry. What it integrates with, not replaces: your LOS. What stays outside the platform: underwriting, rate quoting, and RESPA/TCPA compliance responsibility — those remain yours.
