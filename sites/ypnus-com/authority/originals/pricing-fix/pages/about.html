Why YPN USA exists

I've been in your chair.

“I watched five loan officers chase the same shared lead while the borrower just picked whoever called back first. That’s not a sales problem — it’s a system problem. I built YPN USA so you stop renting your market and start owning it.”— David Moore, 25-year loan officer & founder, YPN USA

I've worked through every rate cycle, refi wave, and purchase market shift since the late 1990s — originating at Wells Fargo Home Mortgage and JPMorgan Chase, two of the largest banks in the world, before going independent and writing “Top 9 Online Real Estate Leads Even the Gurus Do Not Know About”, an Amazon-published guidebook in the Young Professionals Network Real Estate Secrets series — the same YPN this platform is named for. The pattern never changed: the loan officers who struggled weren't short on effort. Their demand source was broken.

Shared leads turn every inquiry into a speed race. Relying on one or two Realtors puts your calendar in someone else's hands. Follow-up slips when you're in front of borrowers. And stitching together a website, a CRM, social posts, and a Google profile eats the hours you should spend closing.

YPN USA is the system I wanted for my own business: an AI assistant that works alongside you, local visibility under your name, follow-up that doesn't depend on your memory, Realtor co-marketing, and a ZIP that's yours on the platform.

My promise: if the tools don't make your next decision easier, walk away. No hard feelings — cancel anytime, no contracts.
Straight answers

What YPN USA is — and what it isn't

A lot of vendors promise loan officers the world. Here's exactly what you're getting.

YPN USA is

  • A mortgage growth platform built for licensed loan officers
  • Your own AI assistant, Cerebro™, to engage and qualify inquiries
  • Local pages, borrower funnels, and Google Business Profile support under your brand
  • Automated follow-up that keeps conversations moving
  • Realtor co-marketing tools that give agents a reason to work with you
  • One protected ZIP per paid plan — not assigned to another MLO on the platform

YPN USA isn't

  • A lead vendor that resells the same contacts to multiple originators
  • A lender, and nothing here is a loan offer
  • A replacement for your CRM or loan origination system
  • A source of licensed mortgage advice — you stay the licensed professional
  • A guarantee of leads, closings, rankings, or referrals

Questions

About YPN USA: common questions

Who founded YPN USA?

David J. Moore, MBA, a mortgage loan officer with 25 years of production experience (NMLS #787257). He built YPN USA around the problems loan officers deal with every day: inconsistent lead flow, manual follow-up, fragmented marketing, and building a recognizable local business.

Is YPN USA a lender?

No. YPN USA provides marketing technology and territory tools to licensed mortgage loan officers. It is not a lender, and nothing on this site is a loan offer.

Is YPN USA a lead company?

No. YPN USA is not a lead vendor that resells contacts. It provides the tools and infrastructure to build your own local source of opportunities. No results are guaranteed.

Who is YPN USA for?

Licensed mortgage loan officers, especially solo and small-team producers who want their own local visibility, faster follow-up, and stronger Realtor relationships without buying shared leads.

What does an exclusive ZIP mean?

One loan officer per ZIP on the platform: YPN USA does not assign that ZIP to another MLO while your paid plan is active. Borrowers remain free to choose any lender. Each paid plan includes one ZIP; additional ZIPs are a separate paid upgrade, subject to availability.

Can I start free?

Yes. The free plan lets you explore Cerebro and check ZIP availability with no credit card. It doesn't include a protected ZIP. Paid plans are Growth at $99.99/month, Pro at $199.99/month, and Elite at $299.99/month, each with a 15-day trial.

Where is YPN USA based and how do I reach you?

YPN Inc. is headquartered at 247 N L Street, Dinuba, CA 93618. Call 559-512-0372 or email support@ypnus.com.
