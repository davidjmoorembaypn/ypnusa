The Core Difference Between YPN USA and Homebot

Homebot is a client-retention tool: it sends past clients and database contacts monthly home-value and equity snapshots to keep your name in front of them for a future refinance or referral. It's built for people already in your book of business. YPN USA is built for the other end of the funnel — generating new, exclusive borrower leads in a ZIP territory through local SEO and AI intake, for loan officers who need new demand, not just retention.
