The Core Difference Between YPN USA and Velocify

Velocify (now part of Ellie Mae/ICE Mortgage Technology) is a sales automation and CRM platform designed to help you work the leads you already have more efficiently. It's a powerful tool — for a lead pipeline that already exists. YPN USA solves the upstream problem: generating exclusive mortgage leads through organic search so you have something to put into your CRM.
