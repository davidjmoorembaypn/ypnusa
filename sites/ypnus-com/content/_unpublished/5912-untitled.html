Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Cerebro?

Cerebro is YPN USA's AI growth assistant. It qualifies borrowers in conversation and helps keep follow-up moving automatically.

What does protected ZIP mean?

One licensed loan officer per ZIP code. Your local visibility in that ZIP isn't shared with another MLO on the platform.

When is my ZIP protected?

Checking availability is free and instant, and doesn't reserve anything. Your ZIP becomes protected once you're on a paid plan.

Can I start free?

Yes. The Free plan lets you explore Cerebro with no credit card required. It doesn't include a protected ZIP.

Can I add more ZIPs?

Yes. Additional ZIP territories are available as a separate paid add-on, subject to availability.

Do I need to replace my LOS/CRM?

No. YPN USA works alongside your existing loan origination system and CRM — it's a growth layer, not a replacement for them.
