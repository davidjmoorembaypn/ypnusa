Common Questions

Know what you're activating.

What does "exclusive ZIP territory" mean?

YPN USA assigns one licensed mortgage loan officer per available ZIP territory. Opportunities generated through the platform in that territory route to that member and aren't shared with another MLO.

Can I check a ZIP before paying?

Yes. Checking availability is free and instant, and doesn't reserve anything. Your ZIP becomes protected once you're on a paid plan.

What does Cerebro actually do?

Cerebro is YPN USA's AI growth assistant. It qualifies borrowers in conversation, keeps follow-up moving automatically, and helps surface what needs attention across your pipeline.

Do I need technical skills to use it?

No. The platform is designed for loan officers, not developers — setup, territory, and follow-up all run without any code.

Does YPN USA guarantee leads or closings?

No. YPN USA gives you exclusive access to the demand your territory and marketing generate — it isn't a shared-lead vendor and doesn't guarantee volume or outcomes.
