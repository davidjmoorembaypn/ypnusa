Day 4: The Realtor Partnership Playbook

One co-branded page = one referral partner = 3–6 closed loans per year

Here's what most MLOs get wrong with Realtors.

They call, they email, they drop off donuts. The agent smiles, says "sure, send me clients," and then refers to someone else. Why? Because there was no system, no shared asset, no reason to make it exclusive.

The co-branded page changes the dynamic. Instead of asking for referrals, you're offering the agent a free marketing tool — a page that promotes both of you to buyers in their market. Now you're a partner, not a vendor.

How to use it this week

1️⃣

Pick one Realtor you want to partner with

The best first target: an active agent in your top city who posts on Instagram but doesn't have a co-branded mortgage partner yet.

2️⃣

Build the page in 2 minutes

Enter their name, brokerage, phone, and photo. The page generates instantly with dual lead capture.

3️⃣

Send this message:

"Hey [Agent name] — I built us a co-branded page that captures buyer leads for both of us. No cost to you, takes 60 seconds to review. Want me to send the link?"

📊 MLOs using co-branded pages get a 3× higher response rate from agents vs cold outreach — because the offer has immediate value.
