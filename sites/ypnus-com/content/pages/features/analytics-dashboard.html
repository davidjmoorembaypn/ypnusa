Analytics Dashboard — See Exactly Which Pages Are Generating Your Leads

Most loan officers have no idea which of their marketing efforts are actually producing pipeline. YPN USA gives you a clear, real-time analytics dashboard that shows you where your leads are coming from, which city pages are ranking, and which follow-up sequences are converting — so you invest more in what works.

What the YPN USA Analytics Dashboard Shows

  • Lead source breakdown: Which city page, loan-type page, or lead magnet generated each lead
  • Organic rank tracking: See where each of your local SEO pages is ranking in Google — and track improvement over time
  • Conversion rate by page: Which pages are getting traffic but not converting — and which are outperforming
  • AI follow-up performance: Open rates, response rates, and appointment bookings from your automated sequences
  • Realtor referral tracking: Which agent partners are sending leads and at what conversion rate
  • Lead velocity: Week-over-week and month-over-month pipeline growth — see the compound effect of your SEO investment

Data-Driven Pipeline Management

Without data, marketing is guesswork. The YPN USA dashboard turns your pipeline into a system you can optimize — identifying underperforming pages to improve, high-converting lead magnets to expand, and agent relationships generating the most referrals. Every decision is backed by actual lead data, not intuition.

YPN USA connects to Google Search Console to pull your actual keyword rankings, impressions, and click-through rates — showing you exactly which search terms are driving traffic to your local pages and where ranking improvements will have the highest ROI.
