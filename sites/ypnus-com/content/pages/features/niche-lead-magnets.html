Niche Lead Magnets That Convert Borrowers

Traffic without conversion is just noise. YPN USA deploys mortgage-specific lead magnets that capture visitor information and deliver value — turning anonymous website traffic into named, phone-verified leads.

Lead Magnets Included in Every YPN USA Plan

🧮 Down Payment Calculator

Interactive tool showing buyers exactly how much they need to save for different loan types (FHA 3.5%, conventional 3–20%, VA 0%). Captures email on results delivery — high-intent leads.

⚡ "Qualify in 60 Seconds" Quiz

5-question pre-qual quiz (credit range, income, purchase price, timeframe, loan type). Delivers a "You May Qualify!" result and routes to your calendar or chatbot. Converts at 18–24%.

📉 Rate Alert Opt-In

Borrowers enter their target purchase price and opt in to rate alert emails. You stay top of mind when rates drop. Builds a warm nurture list automatically over time.

📋 First-Time Buyer Checklist

Downloadable PDF guide covering the home buying process from pre-approval through closing. Exchanges for name + email + phone. Perfect for FHA and first-time buyer pages.

🏠 Local Market Report Opt-In

City-specific market snapshot (median home price, days on market, inventory) delivered via email. Attracts move-up buyers and investors tracking local conditions. Hyper-local by design.

💬 24/7 AI Pre-Qual Chatbot

Available on every page, every hour. Answers borrower questions, collects contact information, and routes hot leads to your calendar — while you sleep, close, or prospect elsewhere.

Why Niche Lead Magnets Outperform Generic Forms

Generic "Contact Me" forms convert at 1–2%. Niche mortgage lead magnets convert at 8–24% because they deliver immediate value — a calculation, a result, a resource — before asking for contact info. Every YPN USA plan includes all six lead magnets above, deployed and tested.
