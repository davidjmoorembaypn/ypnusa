The 12-element checklist high-converting mortgage landing pages use

  1. One clear promise in the headline (city + outcome).
  2. A single goal — one form, one call to action, no menu clutter.
  3. The form above the fold, visible without scrolling.
  4. Only the fields you need — name, phone, email.
  5. A low-friction promise ("60 seconds, no credit impact").
  6. Trust badges — reviews, credit options, speed.
  7. Local language — name your city and programs.
  8. Loan-type clarity (FHA, VA, Conventional, Jumbo).
  9. Mobile-first layout — most borrowers are on phones.
  10. Required compliance — your name and NMLS number.
  11. Fast load — no heavy sliders or pop-ups.
  12. Instant follow-up — respond in minutes, because the first lender to reply usually wins.
