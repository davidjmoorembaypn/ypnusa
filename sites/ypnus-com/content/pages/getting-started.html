Your site is live — now get your first leads

Work these steps over your first two weeks. Most take 10–20 minutes. The chat assistant can help with any of them.

1

Personalize your site

Add your headshot, a short story, and your “why.” Borrowers choose the loan officer they trust — make it sound like you.

2

Optimize your Google Business Profile

The #1 free local lead source. Follow the GBP checklist (about 20 minutes).

3

Share your new site

Put the link in your email signature, text past clients, and post it on social. Your warmest leads already know you.

4

Lock in one Realtor partner

One good agent relationship = a steady pipeline. Start with how to approach a Realtor.

5

Post your first content

Consistency builds trust + traffic. Grab one of these 30 post ideas and publish today.

6

Respond to every lead fast

Your site captures leads 24/7 and auto-replies instantly. The first lender to follow up usually wins.

7

Ask for your first review

Reviews lift your local rankings. Here’s how to get Google reviews.

Your first 7 days

Day 1: personalize + share your site. Day 2: optimize your Google Business Profile. Day 3: reach out to one agent. Day 4–7: post content, ask for a review, and let the automated follow-up work. You’ll have momentum by week two.
