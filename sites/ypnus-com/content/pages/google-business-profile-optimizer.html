A Google Business Profile optimizer should tell you what to fix and what to write, not just hand you a number. The YPN USA GBP optimizer does that for loan officers and real estate professionals. You describe your business, your market and your services, and it drafts a readiness score, prioritized fixes, a business description, Google Post ideas and local SEO next actions. Run the free GBP audit

Know what this tool is and is not. It works only from the details you type. It does not sign in to Google, read your live profile or change anything on it. You copy the recommendations and apply them yourself.

What the free GBP audit gives you

You fill in a short form: whether you are a mortgage or real estate professional, your business name, your market and your services. A notes box lets you paste your current description, categories or services. The tool then returns plain-text results under eight headings:

  • Score. A 0 to 100 readiness estimate based on how complete the information you supplied is. It is not a Google ranking score and does not predict where you appear on the map.
  • Top fixes. The changes worth making first.
  • Primary category direction. Which kind of category to lean toward.
  • Business description draft. Copy you can edit and paste.
  • Services to emphasize. Which of your services deserve the most space.
  • Three Google Post ideas. Topics for your next posts.
  • Review response guidance. How to answer reviews well.
  • Local SEO next actions. What to do after the profile is tidy.

Built to avoid the usual profile mistakes

The tool is instructed to avoid keyword stuffing, fake reviews, invented business details and ranking guarantees. If you leave a fact out, it is told not to make one up. The other side of that rule matters just as much: the output is only as good as your input, so give it specifics, then read every line before it goes live.

Good inputs make a visible difference:

  • Paste your current description so the tool can edit instead of guess.
  • Name your market precisely, with the city and the neighborhoods you serve.
  • List services in plain words a customer would search for.
  • Run it again after you make changes and compare the two results.

When the results come back, work in order. Apply the top fixes first, then the description and category direction, then the services list. Schedule the post ideas across the following weeks, and decide who will answer reviews and how quickly. A short, repeatable routine beats a perfect profile that nobody touches again.

It is a tool that reviews the information behind a Google Business Profile and recommends improvements: completeness, categories, description, services, posts and reviews. Other names include Google My Business optimizer and local SEO profile optimizer.

It is an estimated readiness score based on how complete the information you provided is. It is not a Google ranking score and does not guarantee any placement in Google Maps or search.
