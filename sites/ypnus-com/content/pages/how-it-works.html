Questions

How it works: common questions

Does checking a ZIP reserve it?

No. Checking is free and doesn’t reserve anything, and neither does a free account. A ZIP becomes exclusive when you start a qualifying paid plan, subject to availability.

What does “exclusive ZIP” mean exactly?

One loan officer per ZIP on the platform. While your paid plan is active, YPN USA doesn’t assign that ZIP to another MLO. Borrowers can still choose any lender.

Do I have to replace my CRM or LOS?

No. YPN USA is a growth layer for visibility, capture, follow-up and Realtor co-marketing. It works alongside the systems you already use.

Does Cerebro talk to borrowers for me?

Cerebro engages visitors, helps qualify inquiries, and keeps follow-up moving. It doesn’t give licensed mortgage advice. It surfaces the conversations that need you.

Is YPN USA a lead vendor?

No. It doesn’t resell contacts. It gives you the tools to build your own local source of business. Results depend on your market and your follow-through, and none are guaranteed.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Paid plans renew monthly after the 15-day trial, and you can cancel online anytime with no calls or fees.
