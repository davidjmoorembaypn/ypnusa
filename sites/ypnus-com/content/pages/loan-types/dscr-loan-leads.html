FAQ: DSCR Loan Marketing for Loan Officers

What is DSCR loan marketing?

It is marketing aimed at real estate investors who are looking for DSCR financing: market-specific pages, straightforward explanations and fast replies.

How do I reach investors with a DSCR loan strategy?

Build pages for the cities where investors buy rental property and show real local knowledge. Investors are experienced, so clear information and quick answers matter more than hype.

What should a DSCR loan platform do for me?

It should publish local pages, capture inquiries and follow up, while keeping every lead exclusive to you inside your ZIP territory instead of passing it to other loan officers.

Can DSCR loan software replace my product knowledge?

No. Software handles visibility and follow-up, while you explain the options and guide each investor. The conversation stays with you.

Who handles compliance on DSCR marketing?

You do. YPN USA is a marketing platform, not a lender, so review investor-facing pages and messages with your compliance team before publishing.
