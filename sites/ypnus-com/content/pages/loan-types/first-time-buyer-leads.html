What first-time buyer Borrowers Need From Their Loan Officer

  • First-time buyers have the most questions — and the most loyalty. Be patient, educational, and thorough. They'll refer everyone they know.
  • Down payment assistance (DPA) programs are your opening. Most FTBs don't know these exist. Run through available programs in their city before they ask a competitor.
  • Pre-approval before house-hunting is a pain point. Position your process as '15-minute pre-approval' vs competitors' multi-day wait times.
  • Realtor co-marketing is essential — first-time buyers almost always work with a Realtor. Partner with agent teams in your market and co-brand your pre-qual pages.
  • Fear of rejection is real. Many FTBs don't apply because they assume they won't qualify. Your job is to remove that fear with a no-commitment eligibility check.
