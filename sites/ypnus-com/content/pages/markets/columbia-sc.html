Columbia Mortgage Leads: Where Midlands Buyers Come From

Columbia's buyers sort into a few clear groups:

  • Government and university households. The state capital and the University of South Carolina are major local employers and anchor neighborhoods such as Shandon, Rosewood, Five Points and Earlewood.
  • Military families. Fort Jackson, a major Army training installation in Columbia, makes VA loan leads a natural specialty for loan officers who market to service members and veterans.
  • Lake and suburban move-up buyers. Irmo, Chapin, Lexington and the Lake Murray area, plus Blythewood and Northeast Columbia, draw families who want more space.
  • Health-care and in-town buyers. Prisma Health and Lexington Medical Center employ clinicians and staff across the metro, and the Vista, Cayce and West Columbia offer in-town options for buyers and investors alike.

Each group finds a lender differently, which is why one generic Columbia page rarely serves them all. A page for Fort Jackson families, a page for Shandon and Five Points, and a page for Lake Murray communities can each speak the language of that buyer, and each can be measured separately. Start with the group you already serve best and let the others follow.
