Milwaukee loan officer marketing: neighborhoods and buyer types

The metro has an unusually varied housing stock, and each type brings its own buyer:

  • Owner-occupants of duplexes, often called Polish flats here, who may live in one unit and rent the other.
  • City neighborhood buyers in Bay View, Riverwest, the East Side and Walker's Point.
  • Inner-ring suburb buyers in Wauwatosa, West Allis, Shorewood and Whitefish Bay.
  • Family and move-up buyers in Waukesha, Brookfield, Oak Creek, Franklin and Mequon.
  • Corporate and campus households tied to employers such as Northwestern Mutual and Harley-Davidson and to Marquette University.
