FAQ: Mortgage Marketing for Loan Officers

What is mortgage marketing?

Mortgage marketing is how loan officers attract, earn and keep borrower and referral relationships. It covers local search, Realtor partnerships, content and follow-up.

Which loan officer marketing should I start with?

Start with the two efforts that compound: local search visibility, meaning city pages and a Google Business Profile, and a repeatable follow-up routine for every inquiry. Add partnerships and social content once those run smoothly.

Where can I find mortgage marketing ideas that fit my market?

The guides in this hub cover local SEO, Realtor partnerships and AI lead follow-up. Pick one, apply it to your own ZIP codes and review the results before adding the next.

What does a mortgage marketing strategy need for compliance?

Every page, post and message that borrowers see should follow the rules that apply to your license and state. Borrower-facing compliance is your responsibility, so have your compliance team review templates before they go out.
