Realtor Partnership Strategy — Build Referrals That Close

One productive Realtor partner can send you 8–15 transactions per year. The MLOs who build those relationships don't beg for business — they make agents look good in front of their clients.

The YPN USA Referral Partnership Playbook

🎯 Lead with Value, Not Asks

Never walk into an agent's office and ask for referrals. Instead, bring something: a buyer pre-qualification they can hand to an uncertain client, a co-branded landing page for their listing, or a local market stats email their buyers will love. Give first — every time.

📱 Co-Branded Digital Tools

Agents refer loan officers who make them look tech-savvy. YPN USA builds co-branded pre-qual pages, open house QR codes, and shared lead capture forms that agents can share with buyers instantly. When their buyers have a great experience, they credit the agent — and keep sending you deals.

📞 The 5-Minute Weekly Touch

Top-producing agents get 20 calls a week from loan officers. Stand out by being the one who never asks for business on the call. Instead: "I had a borrower close this week in your ZIP code — thought you'd want to know inventory is moving fast." Value-first. Always.

📊 Referral Transparency Dashboard

YPN USA shows each agent partner how many of their referrals pre-qualified, how many are in process, and deal statuses — without revealing confidential borrower details. Agents who see their referrals turning into closed loans keep the relationship active for years.

🏠 The Open House System

Offer to sponsor open house materials: co-branded flyers with a QR code that links to a pre-qual form. Every visitor scans it — you get the lead, the agent gets a more engaged showing. After the open house, YPN USA automatically sends follow-up emails to everyone who scanned, from you.

How Many Agents Do You Actually Need?

Math: if each agent partner closes 2 transactions/month with buyers, and you're the preferred lender on 60% of deals, 5 productive agent partners = ~6 closings/month. Focus on depth, not breadth. YPN USA helps you service 10–20 agents with digital tools that would require a full-time marketing hire without them.
