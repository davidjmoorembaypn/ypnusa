Mortgage lead generation, explained

Are these leads exclusive to me?

Within your claimed ZIP territory, yes — YPN USA does not assign that same ZIP to another loan officer while your claim is active. See current availability on the territory page.

How is this different from buying a lead list?

A purchased list is a one-time contact batch, often resold to multiple buyers. YPN USA pairs a protected territory with ongoing AI intake and follow-up, so the relationship continues after the first conversation.

Does this replace my Realtor relationships?

No — the Realtor Partnership System is designed to strengthen them, giving you co-branded material to share with agents in your territory alongside your own inbound pipeline.

How quickly are borrowers contacted?

Immediately. The AI assistant engages borrowers on your pages 24/7, qualifies them and scores each conversation by intent, and automated text and email follow-up starts right away, so no inquiry waits for business hours.

Which loan types do the pages target?

Purchase, refinance, FHA, VA, conventional and jumbo searches in your territory, so you capture borrowers who search by loan program as well as by city.

Do I need my own website?

No. YPN USA gives each loan officer their own site and landing pages, and the leads captured there belong to you.
