FAQ: Loan Officer Website for Mortgage Professionals

What should a loan officer website include?

At a minimum: your NMLS details, a clear way to start a conversation, helpful pages for the markets and loan types you serve, and a layout that works on a phone. YPN USA builds these elements around your ZIP territory.

Is a generic mortgage website builder enough?

A generic mortgage website builder gives you a template, but not local SEO pages, lead capture or follow-up. A site built for loan officers connects those pieces so a visit can become a conversation.

Does the same setup work for a mortgage broker website?

Yes. The approach is the same for a mortgage broker website: a branded site, local pages for the cities you serve and lead capture that routes inquiries to you.

How does the site connect to my exclusive ZIP territory?

Your pages are built for your ZIP codes, and no other YPN USA loan officer holds them. That keeps your mortgage loan officer website from competing with another one on the same platform.

Who is responsible for the compliance of my site copy?

You are. Review disclosures, NMLS information and borrower-facing wording for your state before publishing. You can see a finished example in the MLO site demo.
