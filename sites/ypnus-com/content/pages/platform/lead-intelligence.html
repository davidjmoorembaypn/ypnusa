Lead Intelligence for Mortgage Loan Officers

YPN USA Lead Intelligence helps you spend your time on the borrowers most likely to close. Using consented, first-party signals and AI-powered lead scoring, it ranks and routes the leads who come to you — so the hottest opportunities never slip through the cracks.

How Lead Intelligence Works

When a visitor engages with your site or chatbot, YPN USA captures what they tell you — their goal, timeline, and situation — along with how they engage. It scores each lead by likelihood to convert and surfaces the strongest ones in your dashboard, so you follow up first, with the right message. Borrowers self-identify their situation (buying, refinancing, a divorce buyout, financing an inherited home, self-employed); you respond to people who actually raised their hand.

Lead Intelligence Features

  • 🤖 AI lead scoring — leads ranked by likelihood to need a mortgage
  • 💬 Consented intent capture — your chatbot and forms ask each borrower’s goal and situation
  • 📈 Behavioral signals — engagement on your own site (pages viewed, calculator use, response speed)
  • 🧭 Situation-based routing — first-time buyer, refinance, divorce buyout, inherited home, self-employed
  • 🔒 Exclusive — your leads, never shared with another loan officer

Built on consented, first-party data — designed to support TCPA-compliant, fair-lending outreach. You always contact prospects with their consent.
