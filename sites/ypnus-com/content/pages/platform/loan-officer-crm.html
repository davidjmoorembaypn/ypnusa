YPN USA is not a CRM replacement. It works alongside the CRM and loan origination system you already use, and adds what most CRMs don’t have: a source of new local borrowers, built-in lead capture and fast, mortgage-specific follow-up.

What YPN USA adds to your CRM

  • Built-in lead capture and AI intake: local pages and borrower funnels under your brand capture new inquiries, and Cerebro asks the first questions so you know the loan goal and timeline.
  • Automated follow-up: stage-based text and email follow-up for new inquiries (new inquiry, pre-approval, in process), editable under your own brand.
  • Mortgage-specific lead details: loan type, target close date, referral source and ZIP, instead of generic “deal stage” fields.
  • Local demand in a protected ZIP: each paid plan includes a ZIP that is not assigned to another loan officer on the platform; Exclusive adds ZIP code lockout exclusivity.

What stays in your CRM and LOS

Keep your CRM for the full pipeline, past clients and long-term nurture, and your LOS as the system of record for the loan file. Underwriting, rate quotes and RESPA/TCPA compliance remain yours as the licensed professional.
