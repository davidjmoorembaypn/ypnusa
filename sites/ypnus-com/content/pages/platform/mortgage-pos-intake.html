A phone call and a fillable PDF isn't how borrowers expect to start a mortgage in 2026. YPN USA's borrower portal gives every lead a mobile-first digital application and AI-guided intake, so the first conversation happens on their schedule, not just yours.

What the intake platform does

  • AI-guided digital application - borrowers answer conversational questions instead of a static form; AI intake qualifies the basics before you ever pick up the phone.
  • Document collection built in - income verification and document requests happen inside the same portal, not a separate secure-upload tool.
  • Instant lead record - every submitted application becomes a lead record automatically, staged and ready for follow-up alongside your CRM.
  • Works for every loan type - purchase, refinance, FHA/VA/Conventional, DSCR, and commercial intake flows.

Not a replacement for your LOS

This sits in front of your loan origination system, not instead of it - it's where the borrower relationship starts, then hands off cleanly once the file is ready to move into full processing.
