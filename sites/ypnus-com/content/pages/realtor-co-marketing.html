Realtor Co-Marketing for Loan Officers

The fastest way to a steady purchase pipeline is a productive relationship with real estate agents — and co-marketing is how you build it. Done right, it puts your name in front of every buyer the agent works with, while staying fully compliant.

What Co-Marketing Actually Is

Co-marketing means you and a Realtor jointly promote to a shared audience, each paying your proportional share of legitimate marketing costs. Think co-branded property flyers, shared social content, joint open-house promotion, and first-time-buyer guides with both names on them.

Why Realtors Say Yes

Agents want a lender who makes them look good: fast pre-approvals, proactive updates, and zero last-minute surprises. Co-marketing gives them professional materials they did not have to create — and gives you implied endorsement from a trusted local agent.

Staying RESPA-Compliant

Under RESPA, you cannot pay an agent for referrals, but you can split the genuine cost of joint marketing proportionally. Document every arrangement in writing and have your compliance team review your standard agreement once.

FAQ: Loan Officer Realtor Marketing

What is loan officer realtor marketing?

It is joint marketing between you and a real estate agent to a shared audience, such as co-branded pages, shared content and local promotion. The goal is repeat referrals built on useful materials rather than one-off favors.

What is co-branded mortgage marketing?

Materials that carry both your name and the Realtor's, such as a co-branded landing page with local buyer information. YPN USA provides co-branded landing pages, shared lead views and ready-to-send assets so each partner's audience sees both of you.

How does mortgage realtor co marketing stay compliant?

Under RESPA you cannot pay an agent for referrals, so keep every arrangement in writing, split genuine marketing costs proportionally and have your compliance team review your standard agreement. Only your compliance team or counsel can confirm what is allowed.

What do Realtors look for in a co-marketing partner?

Materials they did not have to build, clear communication and a loan officer who follows through. Co-marketing works when it makes the agent look good in front of their clients.

How do I get started with a Realtor partner?

Begin with one agent you already work with, then reuse the shared pages and assets for the next partner. It helps to check whether your ZIP codes are available so the partnership is built in a market you hold.
