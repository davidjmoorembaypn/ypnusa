Refund Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Monthly plans: cancel anytime. 5-year plans: full refund within 30 days.

Monthly subscription fees are non-refundable, but you can cancel at any time and you won't be charged again. A 5-year prepaid plan can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.

Monthly Plans (Pro, Growth, Exclusive)

  • Every paid plan offers a 15-day free trial to try the tools. If you cancel before the trial ends, you are never charged, so there is nothing to refund.
  • Monthly fees already charged are non-refundable, except as required by law, as set out in our Terms of Service.
  • You can cancel at any time. Cancelling stops all future charges; your plan and ZIP code lock continue through the end of the current billing period. See our Cancellation Policy.

Because our Free plan carries no charge, there is nothing to refund on that tier.

5-Year Prepaid Plans

  • Full refund if you ask within 30 days of purchase.
  • No refunds after 30 days.
  • A refund ends the plan and releases your ZIP code lock, so the ZIP code becomes available to other loan officers.

How to Request a Refund

Email support@ypnus.com from the address on your account with the subject line "Refund Request," including your account email and the date of your purchase. We aim to respond within 1 business day.

Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days. Depending on your bank or card issuer, it may take a few additional days for the credit to post to your statement.

What Happens to Your Content

If a 5-year plan is refunded, your account is downgraded, your ZIP code lock is released, and any SEO pages and marketing assets generated on the platform are archived per our standard cancellation process. We recommend exporting anything you'd like to keep before requesting a refund.

Questions

Reach our support team any time at support@ypnus.com or by phone at (559) 512-0372.
