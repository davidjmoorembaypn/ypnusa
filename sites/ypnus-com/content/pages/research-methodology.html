Every YPN USA comparison follows the same method, so you can judge it for yourself and check our sources.

1. What we compare

Each comparison covers the same sections, in the same order:

  • Quick answer: who should choose which tool, in two or three sentences.
  • At a glance: category, best fit and published pricing.
  • Feature comparison: the capabilities a loan officer would actually use, described in each company's own terms.
  • Pricing: exactly as published, with the source and date. If a company doesn't publish pricing, we say so.
  • Pros and cons for both tools, including YPN USA.
  • Workflow fit: where each tool sits in the five-stage mortgage workflow below.
  • Verdict and FAQ.

2. The five-stage mortgage workflow

Most tools for loan officers are not direct substitutes; they work at different stages. We map every tool against the same five stages:

StageWhat happens
Attract new borrowersCreating new demand in your market: local pages, Google Business Profile, content, Realtor co-marketing.
Respond and qualifyAnswering new inquiries fast and sorting who is ready now.
Nurture the pipelineTexts, emails and calls over weeks or months until a borrower is ready.
Advise on the loanPresenting loan options and costs so the borrower can decide.
Retain past clientsStaying in front of past borrowers for refinance, purchase and referrals.

A tool gets a "Yes" for a stage only when its own website describes doing that job.

3. Sources

  • Competitor facts come only from the competitor's own public pages. We don't use review-site summaries, forum posts or memory for facts about another company.
  • Every comparison lists the exact pages we read and the date we checked them.
  • Quoted phrases are the company's own words.
  • YPN USA facts come from our published pricing and live product.

4. Review schedule

We recheck every comparison at least every 90 days and update the "Reviewed" date. Pricing and features change; always confirm on the company's own site before you buy.

5. Conflicts of interest

YPN Inc. sells YPN USA and publishes these comparisons. We disclose that on every page, list YPN USA's weaknesses, and recommend the other tool when it fits better. No company pays to be included, excluded or ranked. Our full standards are in the editorial policy.

6. Report an error

Email support@ypnus.com with the page and the correction. Companies we compare can do the same for facts about their own products.

Frequently asked questions

Where does YPN USA get competitor information?

Only from each company's own public website, filings or official announcements. Every comparison links its sources and the date they were checked.

How often are comparisons updated?

At least every 90 days, and sooner when a company changes its pricing or product.

Is YPN USA biased in its own comparisons?

We sell YPN USA, so we disclose that on every comparison, list YPN USA's cons, and say when another tool is the better fit.
