What's inside

Nine lead sources most agents never try

Written for real estate agents (and useful for loan officers chasing the same buyers), the book walks through nine online lead strategies the author used in his own production, why most "guru" courses skip them, and how to put each one to work without a big ad budget. The goal is simple: spend less time chasing cold leads and more time with people who are actually ready to buy or sell.
