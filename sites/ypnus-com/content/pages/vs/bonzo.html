Bonzo and YPN USA both serve mortgage professionals, but they do different jobs. This page compares them on features, published pricing and where each fits in a loan officer’s workflow, using each company’s own public information.

Quick answer

Choose Bonzo if your problem is follow-up volume and you already have the leads. Choose YPN USA if your problem is not having enough new local conversations. If you have both problems, YPN USA's Pro plan plus your existing CRM is the lower-cost starting point to test.

What is Bonzo?

Bonzo is an outreach and follow-up platform. In its own words it helps business owners "create conversations at scale" through "simplicity, automation, and authentic outreach." Its pricing page names loan officers, processors, assistants and Realtor partners as users.

What is YPN USA?

YPN USA is a mortgage growth platform for licensed loan officers. It combines Cerebro, an AI growth assistant; local pages, borrower funnels and Google Business Profile support under your own brand; automated follow-up; Realtor co-marketing tools; and a ZIP that is not assigned to another loan officer on the platform while your paid plan is active.

YPN USA is not a lender, not a lead vendor that resells the same contacts, and not a replacement for your CRM or loan origination system. It does not guarantee leads, rankings, referrals or closings.

Pros and cons

Bonzo

Pros

  • Full telephony, including calling, voicemail drops and video
  • Automated email, voice and SMS campaigns for long nurture
  • Integrations with LOS, email and lead sources
  • Clear per-seat price for solo users

Cons

  • Does not describe generating new borrower demand; you bring the leads
  • Team pricing is not published
  • No territory or market protection

YPN USA

Pros

  • Builds local demand under your own name
  • Free plan to start; paid plans published from $99/mo
  • Protected ZIP on every paid plan
  • AI follow-up included with lead capture

Cons

  • Not a full CRM: no dialer or deep LOS sync described
  • Local results take time to build and are not guaranteed
  • Newer platform with fewer third-party reviews

Frequently asked questions

Is YPN USA a Bonzo alternative?

Only partly. Bonzo is an outreach and nurture CRM for contacts you already have. YPN USA focuses on creating new local demand and the first response, with automated follow-up included. They overlap on follow-up but solve different main problems.

Does Bonzo provide mortgage leads?

Bonzo's site does not describe selling or generating leads. It describes capturing, uploading and following up with leads from your own sources, plus a MonitorBase add-on for past-client signals.

How much does Bonzo cost compared with YPN USA?

Bonzo lists its Solo plan at $150 per seat per month; team and enterprise pricing are not published. YPN USA has a free plan, and paid plans at $99, $199 and $299 per month. Prices checked on each company's site on November 5, 2026.

Can I use YPN USA and Bonzo together?

Yes. YPN USA is not a CRM replacement. A common setup is YPN USA for local visibility and first response, and a CRM like Bonzo for long-term pipeline management.

Which is better for a solo loan officer?

If you have plenty of leads but slow follow-up, Bonzo. If you need more new conversations in your own market, YPN USA, starting on the free plan.
