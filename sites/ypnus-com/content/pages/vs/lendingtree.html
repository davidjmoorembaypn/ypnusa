LendingTree and YPN USA both serve mortgage professionals, but they do different jobs. This page compares them on features, published pricing and where each fits in a loan officer’s workflow, using each company’s own public information.

Reviewed November 5, 2026 by David J. Moore, MBA, NMLS #787257. LendingTree facts come from LendingTree’s own website (sources below). YPN Inc. publishes this page and sells YPN USA. How we compare tools.

Quick answer

LendingTree suits lenders who need volume now and have the budget and call capacity to compete for it. YPN USA suits loan officers who would rather build a local source of borrowers that keeps working without paying per inquiry. Some use both while local demand ramps up.

What is LendingTree?

LendingTree runs a consumer marketplace. Its partner page says it has helped "connect customers to financial products like yours for 30 years." Borrowers complete a qualification form and then review loan offers; partner lenders can target the consumers they want and see data in LendingTree's Canopy Lender Portal.

What is YPN USA?

YPN USA is a mortgage growth platform for licensed loan officers. It combines Cerebro, an AI growth assistant; local pages, borrower funnels and Google Business Profile support under your own brand; automated follow-up; Realtor co-marketing tools; and a ZIP that is not assigned to another loan officer on the platform while your paid plan is active.

YPN USA is not a lender, not a lead vendor that resells the same contacts, and not a replacement for your CRM or loan origination system. It does not guarantee leads, rankings, referrals or closings.

Pros and cons

LendingTree

Pros

  • Large, established national consumer brand
  • Volume is available quickly
  • Targeting options and a lender portal for data

Cons

  • Pricing is not published, so cost per loan is hard to estimate up front
  • The borrower reviews several loan offers, so you compete at the point of contact
  • Demand stops when spending stops; nothing builds under your own brand

YPN USA

Pros

  • Demand builds under your name over time
  • Transparent monthly pricing with a free plan
  • Protected ZIP on every paid plan
  • AI follow-up included

Cons

  • Slower to start than buying marketplace volume
  • Local results are not guaranteed
  • Better for local markets than for national volume

  • Choose LendingTree if: Lenders with a sales team and budget that want national purchase and refinance volume quickly and can work leads at speed.

Frequently asked questions

Are LendingTree leads shared with other lenders?

LendingTree's partner page doesn't say. It describes borrowers completing a qualification form and then reviewing loan offers, so expect the borrower to compare more than one lender. Ask LendingTree directly how many lenders each request goes to.

How much do LendingTree leads cost?

LendingTree does not publish lender pricing on its partner pages (checked November 5, 2026). YPN USA publishes its plans: Free $0, Pro $99/mo, Growth $199/mo, Exclusive $299/mo.

Is YPN USA a lead vendor like LendingTree?

No. YPN USA does not resell contacts. It gives you the tools to build your own local source of borrowers and respond to them, and it does not guarantee lead volume.

Can an individual loan officer join LendingTree?

The partner page invites lenders to "join our lender network" but doesn't list eligibility rules. Contact LendingTree for requirements.

Which is faster: LendingTree or YPN USA?

Marketplace leads arrive faster. Local visibility through YPN USA takes time to build, but it does not stop when you stop paying per lead.
