This is not an either/or choice. If your close rate is the problem, MortgageCoach targets it directly. If your number of new conversations is the problem, YPN USA does. Fix the bigger bottleneck first.

What is Mortgage Coach?

MortgageCoach, built by TrustEngine, is a loan presentation platform. In its own words it "turns loan complexity into borrower clarity," with side-by-side loan comparisons and documented conversations. mortgagecoach.com now redirects to trustengine.com.

What is YPN USA?

YPN USA is a mortgage growth platform for licensed loan officers. It combines Cerebro, an AI growth assistant; local pages, borrower funnels and Google Business Profile support under your own brand; automated follow-up; Realtor co-marketing tools; and a ZIP that is not assigned to another loan officer on the platform while your paid plan is active.

YPN USA is not a lender, not a lead vendor that resells the same contacts, and not a replacement for your CRM or loan origination system. It does not guarantee leads, rankings, referrals or closings.

Frequently asked questions

What is Mortgage Coach now called?

Does Mortgage Coach generate leads?

Its pages describe loan presentations, documented conversations, credit triggers and a customer portal. They do not describe generating new leads.

Can I use YPN USA and MortgageCoach together?
