If you are buying for an institution, Total Expert is built for that. If you are a loan officer buying for yourself, YPN USA is the one you can start on your own. Check your company's marketing policy before adding any outside tool.

What is Total Expert?

Total Expert describes itself as "the Customer Operating System for financial services," built for mortgage lenders, banks, credit unions and insurers. Its site says more than 200 financial institutions use it.

What is YPN USA?

YPN USA is a mortgage growth platform for licensed loan officers. It combines Cerebro, an AI growth assistant; local pages, borrower funnels and Google Business Profile support under your own brand; automated follow-up; Realtor co-marketing tools; and a ZIP that is not assigned to another loan officer on the platform while your paid plan is active.

YPN USA is not a lender, not a lead vendor that resells the same contacts, and not a replacement for your CRM or loan origination system. It does not guarantee leads, rankings, referrals or closings.

Pros and cons

Total Expert

Pros

  • Broad platform: marketing, sales, lead management and reporting
  • Built for financial institutions with consent awareness
  • AI Sales Assistant for nurturing and qualifying
  • Widely used by institutions

Cons

  • No public pricing; buying usually goes through a demo and the company
  • Sized for institutions rather than one loan officer
  • Does not describe creating new local demand

YPN USA

Pros

  • Buy and start alone, today, with a free plan
  • Published pricing
  • Local pages and Google Business Profile support under your name
  • Protected ZIP

Cons

  • Not an enterprise system of record
  • Fewer integrations and reporting features
  • Local results take time and are not guaranteed

Total Expert is usually chosen by the lender for all its loan officers. YPN USA is usually chosen by the loan officer for their own market. A loan officer at a Total Expert lender can still use YPN USA for local visibility, subject to company marketing policy.

Who should choose which

  • Choose Total Expert if: Lenders, banks and credit unions that want one system for marketing, sales and customer data across many loan officers, with compliance oversight.
  • Choose YPN USA if: Individual loan officers and small teams who need to build local demand themselves without an enterprise purchase.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Total Expert cost?

Total Expert does not publish pricing; its site offers demos (checked November 5, 2026). YPN USA publishes its plans: Free $0, Pro $99/mo, Growth $199/mo, Exclusive $299/mo.

Can an individual loan officer buy Total Expert?

Its site positions it for financial institutions, so purchases typically go through the lender. YPN USA is sold directly to individual loan officers.

Does Total Expert generate leads?

Its home page describes nurturing, qualifying and managing leads and engaging existing customers, not creating new local demand.

What are Total Expert alternatives for a solo loan officer?

Solo loan officers often look at tools they can buy themselves. For outreach, CRMs like Bonzo or BNTouch; for new local demand, YPN USA. See our comparison hub for each.

Can I use YPN USA if my lender uses Total Expert?

Usually yes, since YPN USA focuses on local visibility rather than replacing the lender's CRM. Confirm with your compliance team first.
