What is Velocify?

Velocify by ICE is "a sales acceleration platform built for mortgage lenders." It retrieves, routes and prioritizes leads from many sources and helps sales teams follow up, with products for high-volume lenders, retail loan officers and Salesforce teams.

What is YPN USA?

YPN USA is a mortgage growth platform for licensed loan officers. It combines Cerebro, an AI growth assistant; local pages, borrower funnels and Google Business Profile support under your own brand; automated follow-up; Realtor co-marketing tools; and a ZIP that is not assigned to another loan officer on the platform while your paid plan is active.

YPN USA is not a lender, not a lead vendor that resells the same contacts, and not a replacement for your CRM or loan origination system. It does not guarantee leads, rankings, referrals or closings.

Pros and cons

Velocify

Pros

  • Connects to a very large number of lead sources
  • Routing, prioritization and reassignment for teams
  • Built-in mortgage sales dialer
  • Part of ICE's lending technology suite

Cons

  • Does not generate leads itself; ICE describes lead generation as other products in its suite
  • Pricing not published
  • More than a solo loan officer typically needs

YPN USA

Pros

  • Creates leads rather than only managing them
  • Published pricing with a free plan
  • Protected ZIP on every paid plan

Cons

  • No dialer or team routing
  • Not built for high-volume call centers
  • Local results take time and are not guaranteed

Velocify assumes the leads already exist and helps a team work them efficiently. YPN USA helps a loan officer create them. A lender could feed YPN USA inquiries into a lead manager, but the two solve different problems.

Who should choose which

  • Choose Velocify if: Lenders and call-center teams handling high lead volume from many sources who need routing, prioritization and a dialer.
  • Choose YPN USA if: Loan officers who need their own lead source rather than a system to manage purchased volume.

Verdict

High-volume lender with purchased leads and a sales floor: Velocify. Individual loan officer who needs a source of local borrowers: YPN USA.

Frequently asked questions

Does Velocify generate mortgage leads?

No. ICE describes Velocify as helping lenders "capture, organize, prioritize and act on mortgage leads," and lists lead generation under other products in its suite.

How much does Velocify cost?

ICE does not publish Velocify pricing (checked November 5, 2026). YPN USA publishes its plans from $0 to $299 per month.

Is Velocify good for a solo loan officer?

Velocify is built for mortgage lenders and sales teams, including a version for distributed retail loan officers. A solo loan officer without high lead volume may not use most of it.

Can YPN USA replace Velocify?

No. YPN USA has no dialer or team routing. It is a source of local demand with follow-up, not a lead management system.
