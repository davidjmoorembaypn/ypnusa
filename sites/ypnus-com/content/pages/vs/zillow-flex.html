What is Zillow Flex?

Zillow Flex is "Zillow's performance-based program" for real estate agents, brokers and teams. Agents receive client connections from Zillow at no upfront cost and "pay nothing to Zillow until they successfully close a transaction." Zillow's Flex page also promotes Zillow Home Loans as a lending partner for those clients.

What is YPN USA?

YPN USA is a mortgage growth platform for licensed loan officers. It combines Cerebro, an AI growth assistant; local pages, borrower funnels and Google Business Profile support under your own brand; automated follow-up; Realtor co-marketing tools; and a ZIP that is not assigned to another loan officer on the platform while your paid plan is active.

YPN USA is not a lender, not a lead vendor that resells the same contacts, and not a replacement for your CRM or loan origination system. It does not guarantee leads, rankings, referrals or closings.

Cons

Frequently asked questions

Can mortgage loan officers join Zillow Flex?

Zillow's Flex page describes the program for agents, brokers and teams. Loan officers are not listed as eligible (checked November 5, 2026).

How does Zillow Flex work?

Zillow provides client connections to agents at no upfront cost, and agents pay Zillow only after they close a transaction with one of those connections. Zillow does not publish the fee amount on that page.

Do Zillow Flex agents have to use Zillow Home Loans?

Zillow's Flex page promotes Zillow Home Loans as a lending partner for agents' clients. It doesn't say agents are required to use it.

How can a loan officer compete with Zillow Home Loans?

Be visible to borrowers before they reach a portal, respond fast, and give Realtor partners a co-marketing reason to work with you. Those are the parts YPN USA is built for.
