What is BNTouch?

BNTouch is a mortgage CRM "built only for mortgage since 2004," with texting automation, campaigns, two-way LOS sync, opportunity mining on your database and an optional managed "White Glove" service.

What is YPN USA?

YPN USA is a mortgage growth platform for licensed loan officers. It combines Cerebro, an AI growth assistant; local pages, borrower funnels and Google Business Profile support under your own brand; automated follow-up; Realtor co-marketing tools; and a ZIP that is not assigned to another loan officer on the platform while your paid plan is active.

YPN USA is not a lender, not a lead vendor that resells the same contacts, and not a replacement for your CRM or loan origination system. It does not guarantee leads, rankings, referrals or closings.

Frequently asked questions

How much does BNTouch cost?

BNTouch lists $165 per month for a single user and $95 per user per month for 2 to 10 users, with custom pricing above 10 users; Opportunity Mining and White Glove are priced on a call (checked November 5, 2026). YPN USA has a free plan and paid plans at $99, $199 and $299 per month.

Does BNTouch have a free trial?

Its pricing page says no. YPN USA's paid plans include a 15-day free trial, and the Free plan needs no credit card.

Is YPN USA a CRM?

No. YPN USA focuses on local visibility, lead capture and follow-up. It is not a replacement for a mortgage CRM or LOS.

Does BNTouch generate leads?

BNTouch integrates with 100+ lead providers and mines your existing database for opportunities. Its site does not say it sells leads itself.

Can I use YPN USA with BNTouch?

Yes. Many loan officers keep their CRM and add YPN USA for local demand.
