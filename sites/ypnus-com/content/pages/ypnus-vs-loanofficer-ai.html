What is LoanOfficer.ai?

LoanOfficer.ai calls itself "The AI Mortgage CRM Built for Loan Officers." It answers inquiries, books appointments and reactivates your past database with AI, and adds websites, rate alerts and property monitoring.

What is YPN USA?

YPN USA is a mortgage growth platform for licensed loan officers. It combines Cerebro, an AI growth assistant; local pages, borrower funnels and Google Business Profile support under your own brand; automated follow-up; Realtor co-marketing tools; and a ZIP that is not assigned to another loan officer on the platform while your paid plan is active.

YPN USA is not a lender, not a lead vendor that resells the same contacts, and not a replacement for your CRM or loan origination system. It does not guarantee leads, rankings, referrals or closings.

Frequently asked questions

How much does LoanOfficer.ai cost?

LoanOfficer.ai lists Starter at $197/mo for 2 seats, Team at $397/mo and Brokerage at $697/mo, with a one-time $299 setup fee and $1 for the first 14 days (checked November 5, 2026). YPN USA has a free plan and paid plans at $99, $199 and $299 per month.

Does LoanOfficer.ai generate leads?

Its site describes answering inquiries, booking appointments and reactivating your database, with lead-provider integrations. It doesn't describe selling or generating new leads itself.

Which has better AI follow-up?

LoanOfficer.ai's AI follow-up and appointment booking are its core product. YPN USA includes AI follow-up as part of a broader local growth system. If response automation is your main need, LoanOfficer.ai goes deeper.

Can I use both?

Yes. YPN USA can bring in new local inquiries, and a CRM like LoanOfficer.ai can work them and your past clients.
