Categories of Lead Generation Companies

There are five distinct categories of mortgage lead generation companies, and they are not interchangeable. Marketplace aggregators sell volume and shared attention. Exclusive direct generators sell scarcity. Trigger and data providers sell timing. Life-stage providers sell early positioning. Live transfer shops sell conversations. The category determines your cost per funded loan more than any feature the vendor pitches you on a sales call.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the main categories of mortgage lead generation companies?

Organize vendors into five categories: marketplace aggregators, exclusive direct generators, trigger/data providers, life-stage/public record providers, and live transfer shops. Each category has a different pricing model, exclusivity level, and ideal LO profile. Match the category to your pipeline stage and tolerance for competition.

What questions should I ask a mortgage lead vendor before signing?

Ask where the lead data comes from, what the exclusivity guarantee is and how it is enforced, what the refund or replacement policy covers, whether you can see lead source metadata, what the average lead age is at delivery, and how many LOs receive the same lead. If a vendor cannot answer all six clearly, walk away.

What are the red flags when evaluating mortgage lead companies?

Major red flags include no data source disclosure, 'exclusive' claims with no contract language backing them, aggressive volume discounts tied to long contracts, no refund policy on bad data, and pricing that undercuts market rate by 50% or more, which typically means leads are shared or fabricated.

How do I stop depending on lead vendors for my pipeline?

Use vendor leads as one channel while building your own pipeline through life-stage triggering from public records like probate, divorce, and marriage filings. This gives you control over the data, timing, and territory. You own the lead source instead of renting it from a vendor.

What should exclusive mortgage leads cost?

Genuine exclusive mortgage leads typically range from $100 to $250 per lead depending on source and geography. But the more useful metric is cost per funded loan, which should land between $1,200 and $3,500 for a well-run exclusive program. Sticker price is not the price.
